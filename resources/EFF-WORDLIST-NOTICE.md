# EFF Long Wordlist

`eff-long-wordlist.txt` is the unmodified EFF Long Wordlist, by the Electronic
Frontier Foundation. It contains 7,776 entries and is bundled for offline use.

- Source: https://www.eff.org/files/2016/07/18/eff_large_wordlist.txt
- Description: https://www.eff.org/deeplinks/2016/07/new-wordlists-random-passphrases
